# Tri-Signal Concept

Tri-Signal uses three transparency cuts that move inward through the assembled GenLayer mark.

The source concept is:

1. a lower-left signal travels toward the center;
2. a lower-right signal follows on a delayed window;
3. a top signal descends toward the center;
4. after the third signal reaches the center, the core gives a confirmation pulse.

The logo never rotates or breaks apart.

The moving strokes live inside an SVG mask, so the signals appear as cuts through the mark rather than strokes drawn on top of it.
